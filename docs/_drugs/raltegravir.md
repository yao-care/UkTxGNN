## Raltegravir: From HIV Infection to Simian Immunodeficiency Virus Infection

## One-Sentence Summary

Raltegravir is an HIV-1 integrase strand transfer inhibitor, originally used to treat HIV infection and AIDS (as recorded in the knowledge graph).
The TxGNN model predicts it may be effective for **simian immunodeficiency virus (SIV) infection**, an animal model of HIV rather than a human disease.
There are **no registered clinical trials** and **15 publications** (mostly preclinical or in vitro) related to this prediction, and not all of them directly test raltegravir against SIV.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Based on known pharmacology, raltegravir belongs to the integrase strand transfer inhibitor (INSTI) class. These drugs block the step at which viral DNA is inserted into the host cell genome.

SIV is a close lentiviral relative of HIV with a structurally conserved integrase enzyme, so activity against SIV is mechanistically plausible. Macaque studies of SIVmac251 have used raltegravir as part of antiretroviral regimens and have described viral suppression and the emergence of resistance mutations.

SIV infection is a research model of HIV in non-human primates, not a human disease. This prediction therefore mainly confirms the existing HIV use and does not represent a new human repurposing opportunity.

## Safety Considerations

- **Relations recorded in the knowledge graph (not label text):** the knowledge graph links raltegravir to the following conditions as contraindication relations: myopathy, muscular disease, skeletal muscle disease, familial periodic paralysis, rhabdomyolysis and nodular fasciitis. These should be verified against the SmPC.
- **Drug Interactions:** 36 interactions were identified in total. The main ones are:
  - **Major:** zinc sulfate, zinc acetate, aluminium hydroxide, calcium carbonate (polyvalent cations that may reduce raltegravir absorption)
  - **Moderate:** magnesium oxide, magnesium sulfate, magnesium chloride, orlistat
  - **Minor:** several acid-suppressing drugs (e.g. famotidine, ranitidine, omeprazole, pantoprazole), plus pioglitazone and dexamethasone

Please refer to the SmPC and BNF for full safety information. Report suspected adverse reactions via the Yellow Card Scheme.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
SIV infection is an animal model of HIV, not a human indication, so the prediction adds little new for UK patients. Under the evidence rules, L4 requires a study directly addressing the predicted disease. The macaque studies (e.g. PMID 20233398) do address SIV infection, but the evidence is entirely preclinical, there are no clinical trials, and several of the cited papers do not directly test raltegravir against SIV.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Confirmation of the UK licence indication text, dosage form and authorisation number
- Mechanism of action data to be recorded formally
- Safety information from the SmPC, including a review of the muscle-related contraindication relations
- A clear human or clinical rationale; otherwise this prediction is best treated as a research-model finding only
