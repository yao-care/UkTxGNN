## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Capreomycin is a cyclic peptide antibiotic that is generally understood to act by binding bacterial ribosomes and inhibiting protein synthesis.

Gout is driven by urate crystal-induced inflammation, which has no obvious connection to this antibacterial action. No mechanistic link has been established. The high TxGNN score (0.9991) is a model-based output only, so this prediction should be treated as a hypothesis, not as a reasoned repurposing opportunity.

Capreomycin is also associated with nephrotoxicity and electrolyte disturbance (such as low potassium and magnesium). These are potential concerns in a condition where renal urate handling matters.
