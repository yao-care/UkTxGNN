## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data are not available in the data compiled for this report. Published reviews, however, describe bupropion as inhibiting the reuptake of norepinephrine and dopamine, without serotonergic activity.

This catecholaminergic action overlaps with established ADHD treatments such as methylphenidate and atomoxetine. Meta-analyses and reviews list bupropion as a second-line or nonstimulant option for adult ADHD, which fits a plausible mechanism for this prediction.

Guardrails apply: bupropion lowers the seizure threshold, can affect blood pressure, and carries potential for misuse in people with substance use disorders.

## UK Market Information

One valid single-ingredient marketing authorisation is recorded for bupropion: a modified-release 150 mg tablet. The licence record carries no dosage form or approved indication text, so details are not shown here. Please refer to the SmPC and BNF for authorised uses.

## Safety Considerations

- **Contraindications and warnings:** label-based warnings and contraindications are not available in the data compiled for this report. Please consult the SmPC and BNF. Seizure risk and blood pressure effects are the main concerns noted in the literature for this repurposing.
- **Drug Interactions:** the interaction screen returned 100 interacting drugs. Examples rated Major include:
  - Opioids: fentanyl, alfentanil, codeine, dihydrocodeine and tramadol
  - Others: lidocaine, methylphenidate, hydrocortisone, aldesleukin and amantadine

  Moderate examples include alprazolam, nifedipine, hydrochlorothiazide, cetirizine, chlorpheniramine, acebutolol, alfuzosin, alpelisib and ethanol.

Report suspected adverse reactions via the Yellow Card Scheme.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Proceed with Guardrails**

**Rationale:**
A completed Phase 3 placebo-controlled trial, a Phase 2/3 trial, and a Cochrane review directly address bupropion in ADHD. Only one completed Phase 3 RCT is on record, and the comparator and population details need verification, so the evidence does not reach the highest level. Seizure risk, blood pressure effects and misuse potential call for careful patient selection.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Verification of NCT00048360 design details and results, and confirmation of the bupropion arm in NCT00061087
- Detailed mechanism of action data
- SmPC-based warnings and contraindications review, particularly seizure history, hypertension and substance use disorders
- Review of the major interactions, especially with opioids and other CNS-active drugs
- A monitoring plan for blood pressure and seizure risk in the target population
