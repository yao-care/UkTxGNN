## Why Is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism-of-action data is not available in this evidence pack (`original_moa: [Data Gap]`). Based on generally known pharmacology, eplerenone is a selective mineralocorticoid receptor antagonist, mechanistically related to spironolactone but with reduced anti-androgenic activity; it is established for hypertension and chronic heart failure.

Aldosterone signalling has been implicated in vascular remodelling and fibrosis, which provides a plausible, class-level rationale for exploring mineralocorticoid receptor antagonism in pulmonary vascular disease. However, none of the 20 literature items retrieved for this prediction actually discuss eplerenone or mineralocorticoid receptor antagonists — they are general reviews and mechanistic papers on hypoxia biology (e.g. hypoxia in brain ageing, tumour biology, cognitive impairment, headache), most of which are not specific to pulmonary hypertension. The mechanistic plausibility here should therefore be treated as a hypothesis generated by the TxGNN knowledge graph, not as evidence drawn from drug-specific studies.
