## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Letrozole belongs to the aromatase inhibitor class, which lowers oestrogen production. If a tumour is hormone receptor-positive, aromatase inhibition is plausible as a treatment approach.

This reasoning is an extension of the mechanism used in hormone-sensitive breast tissue disease. It is not specific to nipple carcinoma, and no study in the compiled data tests letrozole in this condition. The prediction should therefore be regarded as a hypothesis only.
