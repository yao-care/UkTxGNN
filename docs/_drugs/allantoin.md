## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data are not currently available for allantoin. It is known as a topical keratolytic and wound-healing agent whose epithelial and anti-irritant actions are used in skin care.

The data compiled for this report do not support a mechanistic link between these actions and retinal microvascular disease. Diabetic retinopathy is driven by damage to the retinal blood vessels, and a topical skin agent has no established activity there. The high TxGNN score (0.9956) is a computational output only and is not corroborated by any trial or publication. On present information the prediction should be treated as speculative.
