## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available (flagged as a data gap in this evidence pack). Based on the pharmacological class information accompanying the prediction, Labetalol is a mixed alpha-1/non-selective beta-adrenergic antagonist, which lowers systemic blood pressure and reduces renal vascular resistance.

Malignant hypertensive renal disease is characterised by acute renal arteriolar necrosis driven by severe, uncontrolled hypertension. There is a plausible pharmacological rationale for a blood-pressure-lowering agent to be relevant to this condition — this is the basis of the TxGNN association. However, this evidence pack does not confirm what Labetalol's original licensed indication actually is, nor does it provide any direct trial or literature evidence connecting Labetalol specifically to this renal condition. The mechanistic link should therefore be treated as a hypothesis generated by the prediction score, not as evidence of clinical efficacy.
