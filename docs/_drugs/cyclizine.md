## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data are not available in the data compiled for this report. Based on known pharmacology, cyclizine is a first-generation H1-antihistamine. Urticaria is a histamine-mediated condition, so blocking H1 receptors is biologically plausible as a way to reduce itch and wheals.

The proposed use sits close to the allergic conditions for which antihistamines are generally used. However, the support comes only from the TxGNN prediction. In clinical practice, non-sedating second-generation antihistamines are generally preferred for urticaria, and no cyclizine-specific data were found, so the prediction remains a hypothesis.
