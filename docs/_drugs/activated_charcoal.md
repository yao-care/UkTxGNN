## Safety Considerations

- **Drug Interactions**: The data compiled for this report list 76 potential interactions. Most of those shown are of moderate severity, and one (acetylcysteine) is minor. Examples include:
  - Acetaminophen (paracetamol) and acetylsalicylic acid (aspirin)
  - Digoxin
  - Tricyclic antidepressants (amitriptyline, clomipramine, desipramine, doxepin)
  - Carbamazepine and valproic acid
  - Tetracyclines (doxycycline, minocycline, tetracycline)
  - Theophylline and dyphylline
  - Acarbose, chlorpropamide, prochlorperazine and amobarbital

  Charcoal can reduce the absorption of co-administered oral medicines, so dose timing should be considered.

Please refer to the SmPC and BNF for warnings and contraindications. Report suspected adverse reactions via the Yellow Card Scheme.
