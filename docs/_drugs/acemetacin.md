## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Based on its drug class, acemetacin is an NSAID and a prodrug of indomethacin. It is expected to inhibit COX-1 and COX-2 and so reduce prostaglandin-mediated inflammation and pain. This is a plausible link to symptom management in osteoarthritis, but it is inferred from drug class rather than from supplied data.

There is an important caveat. The predicted indication is framed as "susceptibility", which describes genetic or risk predisposition rather than a treatable clinical condition. Symptomatic benefit from an NSAID in osteoarthritis would not show that the drug changes a person's susceptibility to the disease. The very high TxGNN score is a computational prediction only, and it should not be read as evidence of clinical benefit.
