## Safety Considerations

Please refer to the SmPC and BNF for safety information. Report suspected adverse reactions via the Yellow Card Scheme.

Two points from the evidence are relevant to safety. In HoFH, administering cholesterol would be expected to worsen the disease. In nonhuman primate studies, a high-fat, high-cholesterol diet accelerated SIV disease progression.
