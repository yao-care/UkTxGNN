## Safety Considerations

Please refer to the SmPC and BNF for safety information. Report suspected adverse reactions via the Yellow Card Scheme.

Points relevant to this review: a 1985 in vitro study of a related sclerosant (sodium morrhuate, PMID 4056566) showed damage to red cells and endothelial cells and granulocyte stimulation, which is a concern for any use outside local sclerotherapy, particularly in neonates.
