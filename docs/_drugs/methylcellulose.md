**Drug Interactions** (25 interactions recorded, of which 20 are listed in the data compiled):

- **Minor:** the remaining listed interactions, mainly antidiabetic agents (insulins, sulfonylureas, acarbose, miglitol, nateglinide, repaglinide), plus linaclotide and picosulfuric acid.
