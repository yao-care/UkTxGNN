## Safety Considerations

**Drug Interactions** (25 interactions recorded, of which 20 are listed in the data compiled):
- **Moderate:** lactulose.
- **Minor:** the remaining listed interactions, mainly antidiabetic agents (insulins, sulfonylureas, acarbose, miglitol, nateglinide, repaglinide), plus linaclotide and picosulfuric acid. Bulk-forming agents may slow or reduce the absorption of other oral drugs, so spacing doses is generally advised.

Please refer to the SmPC and BNF for warnings and contraindications. Report suspected adverse reactions via the Yellow Card Scheme.
