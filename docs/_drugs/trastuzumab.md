## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

A structured mechanism-of-action field is flagged as a data gap in this Evidence Pack (DG002). However, one of the linked clinical trial records (NCT01785420) describes trastuzumab's mechanism directly: it is "a humanised monoclonal antibody that acts extracellularly on the erbB-2 receptor," shown "in both in vitro assays and in animals to inhibit the proliferation of human tumour cells that overexpress erbB-2," and is "a potent mediator of antibody-dependent cell-mediated cytotoxicity (ADCC)." This HER2-blocking mechanism underlies trastuzumab's established role in HER2-positive breast cancer, usually combined with chemotherapy, dual HER2 blockade (e.g. pertuzumab), or endocrine therapy.

Progesterone-receptor status is a biomarker used to stratify breast cancer subtypes rather than a mechanism that competes with or excludes HER2-driven signalling. HER2 overexpression and PR positivity frequently co-occur — so-called "triple-positive" breast cancer (ER+/PR+/HER2+) accounts for roughly 5–10% of all breast cancers (PMID 31410192) — and this subgroup is a recognised, actively studied population for anti-HER2 therapy.

Because HER2 amplification drives tumour proliferation largely independent of hormone-receptor status, trastuzumab's antitumour effect via HER2 blockade would be expected to extend to the HER2-positive/PR-positive subgroup. This is supported empirically: several completed Phase 2/3 randomised trials in the Evidence Pack (e.g. NCT01275677, NCT00005970, NCT00667251, NCT04629846) already enrol and demonstrate benefit in HER2-positive populations that include PR-positive patients, and dedicated hormone-receptor-positive/HER2-positive trials (e.g. NCT00134680, NCT02689921, NCT02152943) test trastuzumab specifically alongside endocrine therapy in this subgroup.

This most likely reflects a gap in the underlying regulatory dataset feeding this Evidence Pack (biologics/injectable licence records may not have been captured) rather than genuine market absence. **Before any decision-making, MHRA licence status should be verified directly against the MHRA products database** rather than relying on this pack alone.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Proceed with Guardrails**

**Rationale:**
Evidence strength is high (L1) — multiple completed Phase 3 RCTs (NCT01275677, NCT00005970, NCT00667251, NCT04629846) and supportive literature (e.g. monarcHER, WSG-TP-II, ExteNET) establish trastuzumab's efficacy in HER2-positive breast cancer populations that include PR-positive patients. However, this Evidence Pack lacks the UK-specific regulatory and safety documentation (MOA, SmPC warnings/contraindications, confirmed MHRA licence status) needed to close out a full safety review.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Verify current UK MHRA marketing authorisation status and licence numbers directly with the MHRA product database (the "0 licences" result in this pack is flagged as a Blocking data gap, DG001, and appears inconsistent with trastuzumab's known UK availability)
- Obtain formal mechanism-of-action and SmPC documentation (DG002)
- Obtain SmPC-sourced key warnings, contraindications and drug–drug interaction data to complete the S1 safety screen
- Confirm PR-status-specific subgroup efficacy data, since most trials analyse the broader HER2-positive population with HR status as a secondary stratification factor rather than a primary PR-positive endpoint
