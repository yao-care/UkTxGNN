# Trastuzumab: From HER2-Positive Breast Carcinoma to Progesterone-Receptor Positive Breast Cancer

## One-Sentence Summary

Trastuzumab is a HER2-targeted monoclonal antibody, originally used to treat HER2-positive breast carcinoma.
The TxGNN model predicts it may be effective for **progesterone-receptor positive breast cancer**, with **13 clinical trials** and **20 publications** currently supporting this direction.
The prediction is best understood as a receptor-defined subgroup of the already-approved HER2-positive indication rather than a truly novel use.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data are not available in the data compiled for this report. Based on known information, trastuzumab is an antibody that targets HER2, and its efficacy in HER2-positive breast carcinoma is established.

A subset of progesterone-receptor positive (PR+) breast cancers also overexpress HER2. In these HER2+/hormone receptor-positive tumours, HER2 blockade is already standard care, usually combined with chemotherapy and/or endocrine therapy. The prediction therefore extends the approved indication to a receptor-defined subgroup.

Benefit is expected only in HER2-positive tumours. HER2-negative PR+ tumours are not expected to respond, so HER2 confirmation is essential.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Proceed with Guardrails**

**Rationale:**
A large completed Phase 3 randomised adjuvant trial (n=3,270) and pivotal published RCTs support trastuzumab-based therapy in HER2-positive breast cancer, including hormone receptor-positive disease. The prediction extends the approved indication to a receptor-defined subgroup, so benefit depends on confirmed HER2 positivity.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Mandatory HER2 confirmation before treatment, as HER2-negative PR+ tumours are not expected to respond
- Full SmPC and BNF review of warnings and contraindications, which were not available here
- Cardiac function monitoring plan (baseline and periodic LVEF)
- Mechanism of action data to complete the mechanistic analysis
- Confirmation of the MHRA authorisation details, including product name, dosage form and approved indication text
- Clarification of the interaction profile for commonly combined agents, particularly paclitaxel and warfarin
