## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the evidence pack. Based on known drug class information, nalidixic acid is a first-generation quinolone that inhibits bacterial DNA gyrase, giving it antibacterial activity against certain Gram-negative organisms (e.g. *Shigella* species).

Bacterial conjunctivitis can, in principle, be caused by susceptible Gram-negative organisms, which is the theoretical basis for this prediction. However, none of the available literature directly evaluates nalidixic acid as a treatment for conjunctivitis — the retrieved publications instead describe pathogen characteristics (e.g. *Tsukamurella pulmonis*, *Shigella* isolates) or use nalidixic acid incidentally as a laboratory selective agent (e.g. Columbia nalidixic agar) or resistance marker. This is an indirect mechanistic inference rather than a demonstrated clinical link.
