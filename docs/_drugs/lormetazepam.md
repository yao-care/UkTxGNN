## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data for lormetazepam is not available in the data compiled for this report. It is, however, a benzodiazepine, and benzodiazepines enhance GABA-A receptor signalling. This is the same mechanism through which benzodiazepines are the standard of care for alcohol withdrawal, so a class-level rationale exists.

That rationale does not carry over automatically to lormetazepam. It is a relatively short-acting agent, and longer-acting benzodiazepines are usually preferred for alcohol withdrawal. The high model score is therefore not backed by any clinical evidence specific to this drug.
