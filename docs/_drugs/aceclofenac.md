## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available. Aceclofenac is an NSAID, a class that acts by inhibiting cyclooxygenase (COX) enzymes and so reducing inflammation and pain. Whether this is relevant to the predicted condition is unclear.

Brachyolmia-amelogenesis imperfecta syndrome is a rare inherited disorder affecting the skeleton (particularly the spine) and tooth enamel. An NSAID could at most offer symptomatic pain relief; it would not be expected to modify the underlying genetic disease. No mechanistic link is supported by the data, and the high TxGNN score (0.9989) should be regarded as a model output only, not as evidence of benefit.
