## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Fluorouracil is a thymidylate synthase inhibitor and antimetabolite. It disrupts DNA and RNA synthesis in rapidly dividing cells, which is a plausible approach for a fast-growing sarcoma. Detailed mechanism of action data was not available in the data compiled for this report, so this description reflects general pharmacological knowledge.

The high score comes from the TxGNN model (deep learning) and may reflect similarities the model learned between related diseases, not biological evidence. Standard rhabdomyosarcoma regimens do not rely on fluorouracil, and nothing compiled for this report shows a benefit in this specific subtype.

Other rhabdomyosarcoma-related predictions (rank 2 to 6) have similar scores. The broader "rhabdomyosarcoma" prediction has only indirect, older, low-tier literature. This is why the top-ranked subtype was assessed on its own and rated L5.
