## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Fluorouracil is a thymidylate synthase inhibitor and antimetabolite. It disrupts DNA and RNA synthesis in rapidly dividing cells, which is a plausible approach for a fast-growing sarcoma. Detailed mechanism of action data was not available in the data compiled for this report, so this description reflects general pharmacological knowledge.

The high score comes from the TxGNN model (deep learning) and may reflect similarities the model learned between related diseases, not biological evidence. Standard rhabdomyosarcoma regimens do not rely on fluorouracil, and nothing compiled for this report shows a benefit in this specific subtype.

Other rhabdomyosarcoma-related predictions (rank 2 to 6) have similar scores. The broader "rhabdomyosarcoma" prediction has only indirect, older, low-tier literature. This is why the top-ranked subtype was assessed on its own and rated L5.

## Literature Evidence

Currently no related literature available for botryoid-type embryonal rhabdomyosarcoma of the vagina.

For context, the broader rhabdomyosarcoma prediction (rank 2) has only indirect literature: 1980s paediatric chemotherapy reviews, a nasopharyngeal carcinoma series, and a 2026 single case report ([PMID 42483493](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42483493/)) of a young adult with lingual rhabdomyosarcoma. In that case, cisplatin plus 5-fluorouracil was followed by disease progression. None of these show a fluorouracil benefit.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
The prediction rests only on a model score. No trials or publications address this disease, so the evidence level is L5. Fluorouracil is also not part of standard rhabdomyosarcoma regimens.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Mechanism of action data and a clearer mechanistic link to rhabdomyosarcoma
- Preclinical or retrospective case-series data on fluorouracil in rhabdomyosarcoma
- Label-based safety data (SmPC warnings and contraindications), including DPD deficiency testing considerations
- Confirmation of licensed indications and formulations in the UK (the single listed product is a cream, which is not suited to a systemic tumour)
