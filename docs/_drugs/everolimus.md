## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Everolimus is, however, a recognised inhibitor of mTORC1, a central regulator of cell growth and survival.

Dedifferentiated liposarcoma is typically driven by MDM2/CDK4 amplification, and the PI3K/AKT/mTOR pathway is frequently activated as well. Blocking mTORC1 with everolimus while also blocking CDK4/6 (with ribociclib) targets two complementary proliferative pathways: cell-cycle progression and mTOR-mediated growth and survival. This is a plausible rationale, but everolimus alone has not been shown to be effective in this disease.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
The only clinical evidence directly in liposarcoma is a single-arm Phase 2 combination study with ribociclib, which cannot isolate everolimus's contribution, and no Phase 3 data exist. Safety label data for this product are also missing from the data compiled for this report.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Full results of the ribociclib plus everolimus Phase 2 study, with analysis of everolimus's contribution
- Everolimus-specific efficacy data in liposarcoma
- Mechanism of action data
- SmPC warnings and contraindications for the UK product, plus indication and dosage form details
- A safety and interaction management plan, particularly for CYP3A4-related and antidiabetic interactions
