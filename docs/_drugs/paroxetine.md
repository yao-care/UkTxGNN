## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data for Paroxetine is not currently available in this evidence pack. The prediction rationale notes that Paroxetine belongs to the SSRI (selective serotonin reuptake inhibitor) class, which acts via serotonin reuptake inhibition.

Ohdo syndrome and its variants are rare genetic disorders caused by mutations in genes such as *KAT6B* or *MED12*, characterised by ptosis, developmental delay, and skeletal abnormalities. There is no known biological or pharmacological relationship between serotonergic mechanisms and the genetic pathways underlying Ohdo syndrome.

Although the TxGNN score of 0.991 is very high, this is a pure knowledge-graph link prediction with no supporting mechanistic hypothesis, clinical trial, or literature evidence. The prediction is assessed as a **potential noise link** rather than a credible repurposing signal, and should not be treated as a genuine candidate without further mechanistic investigation.
