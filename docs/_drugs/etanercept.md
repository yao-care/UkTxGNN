## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Etanercept is generally understood to act by binding and neutralising TNF, which reduces TNF-driven inflammation.

Coccygeal hypermobility is usually a mechanical or traumatic condition of the coccyx, often linked to injury, childbirth or abnormal loading. The data show no plausible TNF-mediated mechanism for it. The high TxGNN score (0.9963) is a model output only. It is not backed by any trial, publication or mechanistic evidence, so the prediction should be treated as unsupported at present.
