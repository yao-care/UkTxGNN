## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data for duloxetine are not available in the data compiled for this report. Duloxetine is known to belong to the SNRI class, which acts on serotonin and noradrenaline signalling.

Benign paroxysmal torticollis of infancy is a rare childhood condition, related to migraine and linked to the CACNA1A gene (a calcium channel). No plausible mechanism by which duloxetine would act on this condition is supported by the available data. The high score therefore reflects a model-based association only and should not be read as a pharmacological rationale. The safety of duloxetine in infants has also not been established in the data reviewed.
