## Atazanavir: From HIV Infection to Simian Immunodeficiency Virus Infection

## One-Sentence Summary

Atazanavir is an HIV-1 protease inhibitor, originally used to treat HIV infection and AIDS.
The TxGNN model predicts it may be effective for **simian immunodeficiency virus (SIV) infection**, but this is an animal-model disease, and only **1 preclinical publication** and **no clinical trials** currently support the prediction.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data are not available in the data compiled for this report. Based on known information, atazanavir is an HIV-1 protease inhibitor. Its efficacy in HIV infection is established, and mechanistically it may be applicable to SIV infection.

SIV is a lentivirus closely related to HIV, and its protease is structurally and functionally similar to HIV-1 protease, the known target of atazanavir. The link is therefore plausible.

However, SIV infection is an animal-model disease (non-human primates), so it has limited relevance to human prescribing. The single publication available describes macaques treated with a combination antiretroviral regimen, which is indirect evidence rather than evidence for atazanavir alone. The high TxGNN score is best read as reflecting the close biological similarity between SIV and HIV, not a new human therapeutic opportunity.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
SIV infection is an animal-model disease with no human treatment need, and the only supporting evidence is one indirect macaque study of a combination regimen. The prediction is mechanistically plausible but offers no actionable human repurposing opportunity.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Confirmation of the actual research question (for example, use of SIV models for preclinical work rather than clinical repurposing)
- Detailed mechanism of action data
- Label warnings and contraindications from the SmPC and BNF
- Verification of the UK authorisation record, including dosage form and approved indication

---
