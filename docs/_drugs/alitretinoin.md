## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Pharmacology records show that alitretinoin binds all three retinoic acid receptors (RAR-alpha, -beta, -gamma) and all three retinoid X receptors (RXR-alpha, -beta, -gamma). It also has a recorded interaction with the vitamin K epoxide reductase complex subunit 1 (VKORC1).

Retinoid signalling can influence reproductive tissues, but there is no clear mechanistic link to a therapeutic effect in amenorrhea. Retinoids are teratogenic and may cause menstrual disturbances, so any association is more likely to reflect adverse effects than benefit. The prediction should be treated as a computational hypothesis only.

## Safety Considerations

- **Drug Interactions**: Pharmacology records list binding to RAR-alpha, RAR-beta, RAR-gamma, RXR-alpha, RXR-beta and RXR-gamma, plus VKORC1 (vitamin K epoxide reductase complex subunit 1). These are target interactions rather than graded drug–drug interactions, so no interaction severity levels are available. Because VKORC1 is the target of warfarin, coagulation monitoring is a sensible precaution if co-prescribed; check the SmPC and BNF.
- **General class cautions**: Retinoids are teratogenic and may cause menstrual disturbances; this is relevant to the predicted indication.

Please refer to the SmPC and BNF for full safety information. Report suspected adverse reactions via the Yellow Card Scheme.
