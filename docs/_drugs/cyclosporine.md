## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism-of-action data for Cyclosporine is flagged as a data gap in this evidence pack (DrugBank query pending, DG002). Based on established pharmacology, Cyclosporine is a calcineurin inhibitor that blocks T-cell activation and cytokine production, and is used clinically to prevent solid-organ transplant rejection and to prevent/treat graft-versus-host disease (GvHD) after allogeneic haematopoietic stem cell transplantation (HSCT).

Chronic Granulomatous Disease (CGD) is a primary immunodeficiency caused by defective NADPH oxidase in phagocytes; its established curative treatment is allogeneic HSCT. The clinical trial and literature identified for this candidate do not describe Cyclosporine treating the underlying NADPH oxidase defect. Instead, they describe Cyclosporine's conventional role as post-transplant immunosuppression/GvHD prophylaxis in children undergoing HSCT for non-malignant diseases, of which CGD is one example.

This means the TxGNN association is most plausibly explained by Cyclosporine's supportive role within the standard-of-care transplant pathway for CGD, rather than a direct disease-modifying mechanism against CGD itself. This distinction is important and should be clarified before further evaluation.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
The identified trial and literature support Cyclosporine's established use as GvHD prophylaxis during HSCT — the actual curative therapy for CGD — rather than demonstrating a direct disease-modifying effect of Cyclosporine on CGD. Combined with a Blocking data gap on regulatory safety labelling and the absence of any current UK marketing authorisation, the evidence does not yet support progressing beyond a holding position.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- TFDA/MHRA SmPC warnings, contraindications and drug-interaction data (Blocking gap, DG001)
- Confirmed mechanism-of-action data from DrugBank (High-priority gap, DG002)
- Clarification of whether the CGD association reflects a genuine disease-modifying role or solely Cyclosporine's supportive use as GvHD prophylaxis during HSCT
- Confirmation of a UK supply/licensing route, since 0 marketing authorisations are currently on record
