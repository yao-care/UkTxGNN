## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data are not available in the data compiled for this report. As a benzodiazepine, alprazolam is generally understood to enhance GABA-A receptor signalling, producing sedative and hypnotic effects. That is a plausible pharmacological basis for treating insomnia, particularly insomnia that accompanies anxiety.

This rationale rests on general class pharmacology only. None of the retrieved publications is a controlled trial of alprazolam for primary insomnia. The high TxGNN score is a computational prediction, not clinical evidence.

Benzodiazepine risks weigh against active repurposing without direct evidence. These include dependence, falls, cognitive impairment and a substantial interaction burden, with 100 interactions recorded in the data compiled for this report.
