## Pazopanib: Liposarcoma
{: .text-alpha }

## One-Sentence Summary

Pazopanib is an oral multi-targeted tyrosine kinase inhibitor with anti-angiogenic and antitumour properties, and the TxGNN model predicts it may be useful in **liposarcoma**.
Six related clinical trials, two of them dedicated phase 2 studies in liposarcoma, and 20 publications support this direction. No phase 3 trial exists, and the evidence level is **L2**.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data are not available in the data compiled for this report. The published literature describes pazopanib as a multi-target tyrosine kinase inhibitor with anti-angiogenic and antitumorigenic properties. Its activity against PDGFR and VEGFR signalling is relevant to soft tissue sarcomas.

Liposarcoma is a group of adipocytic soft tissue sarcomas with limited treatment options in the advanced or metastatic setting. Adipocytic tumours were excluded from the pivotal phase 3 pazopanib trial in other soft tissue sarcomas because activity was expected to be lower. Several phase 2 studies in liposarcoma or mixed soft tissue sarcoma have since reported mixed results. Dedicated liposarcoma data are modest, and activity appears to depend on histological subtype. The prediction is therefore plausible, but it needs confirmation in well-designed studies.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Proceed with Guardrails**

**Rationale:**
Two completed phase 2 trials of pazopanib in advanced liposarcoma (NCT01506596 and NCT01692496) and a phase 2 publication place the evidence at L2. No phase 3 trial exists. Activity appears to depend on histological subtype, so further investigation should stay within a research or specialist multidisciplinary setting.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Review of the full results of the two liposarcoma phase 2 trials, with subtype-specific response and progression-free survival data
- Confirmatory evidence, ideally from a randomised study, in defined liposarcoma subtypes
- Mechanism of action data to support subtype selection
- A safety monitoring plan based on the SmPC, including management of the interactions listed above
- Review of the local licence record, which has no recorded indication text
