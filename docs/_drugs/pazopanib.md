## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in this evidence pack (flagged as a High-severity data gap). Based on established pharmacological knowledge, pazopanib is a multi-targeted tyrosine kinase inhibitor acting on VEGFR-1/2/3, PDGFR-α/β and c-KIT, producing combined anti-angiogenic and anti-proliferative effects. This class of activity underpins its established use in vascularised, kinase-driven solid tumours such as renal cell carcinoma and soft tissue sarcoma.

Dedifferentiated and myxoid liposarcoma subtypes frequently show PDGFR-α pathway activation. Pazopanib's direct PDGFR inhibition, combined with VEGFR-mediated restriction of tumour vascular supply, provides a mechanistically coherent rationale for activity in liposarcoma. Notably, the pivotal Phase 3 PALETTE trial — which established pazopanib's soft tissue sarcoma licence — specifically **excluded** adipocytic (liposarcoma) subtypes, meaning this indication represents a genuine evidence gap rather than an on-label extension. Subsequent dedicated Phase II studies (see below) have since attempted to close that gap directly.
