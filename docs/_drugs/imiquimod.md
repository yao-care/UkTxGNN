# Imiquimod: From Actinic Keratosis to Pre-malignant Neoplasm

## One-Sentence Summary

Imiquimod is a topical TLR7 agonist with an established licensed use in actinic keratosis (itself a pre-malignant epithelial lesion), alongside off-label use in other HPV-related and epithelial dysplastic conditions. The TxGNN model predicts a broader class effect across **Pre-malignant Neoplasm**, with **19 clinical trials** and **9 publications** currently supporting this direction, including one completed Phase 3 RCT with 259 patients.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data for imiquimod is currently flagged as a data gap in the source records. However, the evidence pack's own repurposing rationale describes imiquimod as a **Toll-like receptor 7 (TLR7) agonist**: topical application locally induces interferon-α and other cytokines, generating an antitumour and antiviral immune response. This mechanism underpins imiquimod's existing approved use in actinic keratosis — a keratinocyte pre-malignant lesion — as well as superficial basal cell carcinoma and anogenital warts.

Because actinic keratosis is itself classified as a pre-malignant epithelial neoplasm, the TxGNN prediction of broader efficacy across the "pre-malignant neoplasm" category represents a relatively small mechanistic extrapolation rather than a novel therapeutic hypothesis. The strongest supporting evidence comes from lentigo maligna (a pre-malignant melanocytic facial lesion), where a completed Phase 3 RCT (n=259) evaluated imiquimod as neoadjuvant treatment to reduce surgical excision margins. Further trials extend the same TLR7-mediated local immune-activation mechanism to other HPV-driven intraepithelial neoplasias (cervical, vulvar, anal) and to actinic cheilitis and oral premalignant lesions, though evidence quality across these sites is more variable (small sample sizes, early termination).

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Proceed with Guardrails**

**Rationale:**
The prediction is supported by L1-level evidence, anchored by a completed Phase 3 RCT (n=259) in lentigo maligna and a coherent TLR7-mediated mechanism that extends imiquimod's known pre-malignant epithelial indication (actinic keratosis) to related lesions. However, most site-specific trials outside actinic keratosis are small, terminated early, or of unknown status, so guardrails are warranted before broader clinical adoption.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- TFDA/MHRA-equivalent product labelling — warnings, contraindications, and precautions (currently a blocking data gap; source: official regulatory label, to be obtained and parsed)
- Confirmed mechanism of action data from DrugBank or equivalent primary source
- UK marketing authorisation status confirmation, given imiquimod is currently recorded as not marketed
- Site-specific efficacy confirmation for each pre-malignant subtype (cervical, vulvar, anal, oral, lip) given heterogeneous trial quality
- A structured safety review incorporating the malignant-transformation and inflammatory adverse-event case reports identified in related literature searches
