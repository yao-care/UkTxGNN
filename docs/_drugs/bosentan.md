## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data are not available in the data compiled for this report. Bosentan is known as a dual endothelin receptor antagonist, and the rationale below rests on that class property.

Endothelin-1 is reported to contribute to synovial inflammation, vasculopathy and release of inflammatory mediators (TNF-alpha, LTB4, IL-17 axis). In mouse models, bosentan reduced inflammation in collagen-induced arthritis, with a TNF-alpha-dependent component. Selective endothelin receptor blockade also reduced neutrophil accumulation and oedema in zymosan-induced arthritis. These findings give a plausible biological link, but they come from animal models only.

Most other retrieved papers concern pulmonary arterial hypertension in connective tissue disease or systemic sclerosis, not rheumatoid arthritis, and do not support this prediction directly.

**Evidence level note:** No cited study directly examines bosentan in human rheumatoid arthritis, and no trial is registered. Under the evidence rules, the level is therefore L5, even though preclinical studies exist. The preclinical papers are described under Literature Evidence for context.

These are not taken from label text and should be checked against the SmPC.

Please refer to the SmPC and BNF for full safety information. Report suspected adverse reactions via the Yellow Card Scheme.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
The prediction score is high (0.9980), but the supporting evidence is limited to animal arthritis models. There are no registered trials and no human efficacy data in rheumatoid arthritis. The liver-related cautions are also relevant for a chronic inflammatory condition.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Human efficacy and safety data in rheumatoid arthritis, starting with a well-designed early-phase trial
- Detailed mechanism of action data for bosentan
- Review of SmPC warnings and contraindications, with a liver monitoring plan
- Assessment of interactions with drugs commonly used in rheumatoid arthritis, such as corticosteroids
