## Bosentan: From Pulmonary Arterial Hypertension to Rheumatoid Arthritis
{: .text-alpha }

## One-Sentence Summary

Bosentan is an endothelin receptor antagonist, originally used to treat pulmonary arterial hypertension (PAH) and systemic sclerosis-related vascular complications.
The TxGNN model predicts it may be effective for **rheumatoid arthritis**, but there are currently **no registered clinical trials** and **no human efficacy studies**. The supporting evidence consists of **2 animal studies** plus background reviews.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Bosentan is known as a dual endothelin receptor antagonist (ET-A/ET-B), and its efficacy in pulmonary arterial hypertension is established.

Endothelin-1 is raised in the plasma and synovial membrane of people with rheumatoid arthritis. It is thought to contribute to synovial inflammation, vasculopathy and cytokine release (TNF-alpha, LTB4, IL-17 axis). In mice, bosentan reduced inflammation in collagen-induced arthritis, with a TNF-alpha-dependent component. Blocking endothelin receptors also reduced neutrophil accumulation and oedema in zymosan-induced arthritis.

This mechanistic link is plausible, but it rests on animal data only. No human efficacy data for rheumatoid arthritis were identified. Most of the other retrieved papers concern connective-tissue-disease-associated PAH or systemic sclerosis, which are close to the drug's existing area of use rather than rheumatoid arthritis itself.

## Safety Considerations

The knowledge graph links bosentan with contraindication or caution signals for the following conditions (these come from graph relationships, not from label text):
- **Liver:** liver failure, liver disease, drug-induced liver injury, gallbladder disease
- **Bilirubin:** hyperbilirubinaemia, hereditary hyperbilirubinaemia, Gilbert syndrome
- **Haematological:** thrombocytopenia, anaemia, macrocytic anaemia
- **Cardiopulmonary:** congestive heart failure, pulmonary oedema, pulmonary veno-occlusive disease

**Drug Interactions:** 100 interactions were recorded; the first 20 returned are all of Moderate severity. Examples include:
- Corticosteroids (hydrocortisone, dexamethasone, betamethasone, triamcinolone)
- Antidiabetic agents (pioglitazone, acetohexamide, chlorpropamide, and the SGLT2 inhibitors canagliflozin, dapagliflozin, empagliflozin, ertugliflozin)
- Antimicrobials and antifungals (clarithromycin, metronidazole, clotrimazole)
- Others (aprepitant, cimetidine, cisapride, dronabinol, eliglustat, eluxadoline)

Please refer to the SmPC and BNF for the full warnings, contraindications and interaction management. Report suspected adverse reactions via the Yellow Card Scheme.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
Rheumatoid arthritis is supported only by a high model score and mouse studies (L4); no human efficacy data or registered trials were found. The knowledge-graph liver and haematological cautions also warrant care in an inflammatory arthritis population.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Detailed mechanism of action data for bosentan
- Human proof-of-concept data (for example, a pilot study) in rheumatoid arthritis
- Full UK authorisation details (PL number, dosage form, approved indication) and SmPC safety information
- A liver function and haematological monitoring plan suitable for people with rheumatoid arthritis, who commonly take other hepatotoxic or myelosuppressive therapies

### Other Predicted Indications (Brief Note)

- **Limited systemic sclerosis (score 99.65%, evidence level L2):** This is the best-supported of the other predictions, but it overlaps with bosentan's existing use in systemic sclerosis and PAH. The more distinct question is **digital ulcers**: a 2023 systematic review and meta-analysis ([36974107](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36974107/)), a randomised trial of bosentan versus nifedipine for scleroderma vasculopathy ([36278776](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36278776/)) and 2024 Portuguese recommendations ([38956991](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38956991/)) are relevant. An in vitro study ([27716320](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27716320/)) showed bosentan prevents endothelial-to-mesenchymal transition. A small mechanistic study ([19350343](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19350343/)) found no effect on microvasculature in limited cutaneous SSc. The primary trials should be verified before any decision.
- **Gout (99.21%), brachydactyly-syndactyly syndrome, colobomatous microphthalmia-rhizomelic dysplasia syndrome, pseudoxanthoma elasticum (forme fruste), kyphoscoliotic heart disease, elastoma and hypertrichosis:** Model prediction only (L5), with no supporting trials or therapeutic evidence. The single gout-related item is a pharmacovigilance study of endothelin receptor antagonist adverse events, which does not show therapeutic benefit. All are on Hold.
