## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Citalopram belongs to the SSRI class, which blocks reuptake of serotonin and raises serotonergic signalling in the brain.

Serotonergic dysfunction in cortico-striato-thalamo-cortical circuits is implicated in obsessive-compulsive disorder (OCD), and SSRIs are an established first-line pharmacological class for the condition. This class-level rationale is consistent with the very high TxGNN score.

Evidence specific to citalopram is weaker. The citalopram studies identified are small open-label or single-blind studies and case series, and no citalopram-specific Phase 3 randomised trial was found in the data compiled.

## Safety Considerations

**Drug Interactions** (100 interactions recorded; examples of those rated Major):

- Cimetidine
- Clarithromycin
- Cisapride
- Dolasetron

Moderate-level examples include famotidine, loperamide, morphine and acetylsalicylic acid. Several of the Major interactions (such as cisapride, dolasetron and clarithromycin) are of particular concern for QT prolongation or serotonergic effects.

Disease-level contraindication relations were recorded for conditions including long QT syndrome, torsades de pointes, epilepsy, bipolar disorder, liver disease, heart disease, serotonin syndrome and inappropriate ADH syndrome. These are not label text and should be checked against the SmPC.

Please refer to the SmPC and BNF for full safety information. Report suspected adverse reactions via the Yellow Card Scheme.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
The prediction score is very high and the SSRI class is established in OCD. However, no cited trial tests citalopram in OCD with a controlled design; direct evidence is limited to small open-label studies, so the evidence level is capped at L5 under the current criteria.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- A controlled trial (ideally randomised and placebo- or active-comparator-controlled) of citalopram specifically in OCD
- Mechanism of action data for citalopram
- Review of the SmPC and BNF for warnings and contraindications, especially QT prolongation and serotonin syndrome risk
- A plan for managing the Major drug interactions listed above
