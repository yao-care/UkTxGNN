## Amiodarone: Catecholaminergic polymorphic ventricular tachycardia
{: .text-alpha }

## One-Sentence Summary

Amiodarone is an antiarrhythmic medicine used for ventricular rhythm disorders. The TxGNN model predicts it may be relevant to **catecholaminergic polymorphic ventricular tachycardia (CPVT)**, with a score of 0.9978. Support is limited: **no registered clinical trials** and **9 publications** (cohorts, a review and case reports), none of which tests amiodarone against comparators in CPVT.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Amiodarone is generally described as a multichannel blocker with beta-blocking and calcium-channel effects, and these could plausibly bear on the calcium-handling abnormalities that drive CPVT (mutations in RYR2 or CASQ2 causing delayed afterdepolarisations).

However, the retrieved literature indicates amiodarone is generally less effective than beta-blockers, flecainide and sympathetic denervation in CPVT. The high TxGNN score most likely reflects the shared ventricular arrhythmia phenotype rather than a proven treatment effect. None of the retrieved publications evaluates amiodarone as a treatment for CPVT directly, so the prediction is best treated as a research question.

## Safety Considerations

- **Drug interactions** (100 recorded; examples): Major interactions with cisapride, clarithromycin, dolasetron, eliglustat, amphotericin B products, clotrimazole, and corticosteroids such as hydrocortisone and dexamethasone; Moderate with famotidine, cimetidine and aprepitant.

Please refer to the SmPC and BNF for full safety information. Report suspected adverse reactions via the Yellow Card Scheme.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
The prediction rests on a model score alone. No trials exist and no cited study directly evaluates amiodarone in CPVT, while the literature suggests beta-blockers, flecainide and sympathetic denervation are preferred. Amiodarone's safety burden (thyroid, liver, lung, QT effects and many interactions) adds to the case for caution.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Direct comparative or observational data on amiodarone in CPVT
- Mechanism of action data to support the calcium-handling hypothesis
- Label-based warnings and contraindications from the SmPC
- Licence indication text and dosage form for the UK authorisation
