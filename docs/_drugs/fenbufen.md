## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Based on the known class pharmacology, fenbufen is an NSAID and a prodrug of felbinac (biphenylacetic acid), whose active metabolites inhibit COX-1 and COX-2 and reduce prostaglandin-mediated pain and inflammation.

Osteoarthritis is a painful joint condition in which inflammation and pain are important symptoms. A drug that reduces prostaglandin-driven pain and inflammation is mechanistically well suited to it. The literature reviewed describes fenbufen being studied extensively in osteoarthritis of the knee and hip, which is consistent with the model's prediction.

Note that the approved indication text is not recorded in the local licence data, so this report cannot confirm from local records whether osteoarthritis is already a labelled use.

## Safety Considerations

Please refer to the SmPC and BNF for safety information. Report suspected adverse reactions via the Yellow Card Scheme.

Published reports in the literature reviewed also describe rash and pulmonary eosinophilia associated with fenbufen (BMJ, 1990), so hypersensitivity-type reactions should be kept in mind.
