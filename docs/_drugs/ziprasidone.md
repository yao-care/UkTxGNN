## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Ziprasidone is an atypical antipsychotic, and its pharmacology is generally described as dopamine D2 and serotonin 5-HT2A receptor antagonism.

Trichotillomania is a habit and impulse-control disorder, and both dopaminergic and serotonergic pathways are thought to be relevant to such conditions. This is a plausible rationale only. The data compiled for this report contain no study that tests it, so the prediction remains a hypothesis.
