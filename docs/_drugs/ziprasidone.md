## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

According to the Mechanism of Action section of the US FDA-approved prescribing information for ziprasidone: "The mechanism of action of ziprasidone in the treatment of the listed indications could be mediated through a combination of dopamine type 2 (D 2 ) and serotonin type 2 (5HT 2 ) antagonism." ([DailyMed: Ziprasidone Mesylate 20 Mg/Ml (ziprasidone mesylate) prescribing information, Armas Pharmaceuticals Inc.](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/lookup.cfm?setid=c5c27f0f-4e45-4bca-bb9a-2d3f1c243348), label version 2026-09-18; accessed 2026-11-19.) Ziprasidone is an atypical antipsychotic, and its pharmacology is generally described as dopamine D2 and serotonin 5-HT2A receptor antagonism.

Trichotillomania is a habit and impulse-control disorder, and both dopaminergic and serotonergic pathways are thought to be relevant to such conditions. This is a plausible rationale only. The data compiled for this report contain no study that tests it, so the prediction remains a hypothesis.

## Safety Considerations

- **Conditions flagged as cautions for this drug:** the data compiled for this report list long QT syndrome, torsades de pointes, heart conduction disease, sudden cardiac arrest, myocardial infarction, orthostatic hypotension, epilepsy, liver failure, kidney disease, diabetes mellitus and diabetic ketoacidosis, hyperglycaemia, neutropenia and agranulocytosis, and potassium deficiency. These are not taken from label text, so check the SmPC for authoritative wording.
- **Drug Interactions:** 100 interactions were recorded. Those rated Major include hydrocortisone, amphotericin B (including the lipid complex), bupropion and morphine. Moderate interactions include antidiabetic agents (metformin, pioglitazone, acarbose, alogliptin, albiglutide), anticholinergics (atropine, hyoscyamine, glycopyrronium), epinephrine, famotidine, loperamide, lorcaserin and bisacodyl.

Please refer to the SmPC and BNF for full safety information. Report suspected adverse reactions via the Yellow Card Scheme.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
The prediction score is high, but there are no trials or publications that directly study ziprasidone in trichotillomania, so the evidence level is L5. Ziprasidone also carries QT-related cardiac cautions that would need careful management in any new use.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Mechanism of action data for ziprasidone
- Preclinical or early clinical evidence in trichotillomania
- A controlled trial design with ECG monitoring and a plan for interaction screening
- Review of the SmPC warnings and contraindications
