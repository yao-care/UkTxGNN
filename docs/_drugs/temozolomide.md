# Temozolomide: From Malignant Glioma to Adult Astrocytic Tumour

## One-Sentence Summary

Temozolomide is an oral alkylating agent long recognised as standard-of-care chemotherapy for glioblastoma and anaplastic astrocytoma, though no original indication text or UK licence is recorded in this Evidence Pack.
The TxGNN model predicts it may be effective for **Adult Astrocytic Tumour**, with **2 clinical trials** and **20 publications** currently supporting this direction.
Because astrocytic tumours are the same disease family as glioblastoma, this result most likely reflects confirmation of an already-established use rather than a genuinely new repurposing signal.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in this Evidence Pack (Data Gap DG002), and no original indication is recorded in the regulatory dataset used to build this pack (`original_indications` is empty and no UK licences are on file). Based on well-established pharmacological knowledge, temozolomide is an oral second-generation alkylating agent of the imidazotetrazine class. It is spontaneously hydrolysed to the active metabolite MTIC, which methylates guanine residues in tumour DNA, ultimately triggering apoptosis in dividing cells. It is internationally recognised as a cornerstone treatment for glioblastoma multiforme and anaplastic astrocytoma.

Because "adult astrocytic tumour" is essentially the same disease category as glioblastoma/anaplastic astrocytoma, this TxGNN prediction should be interpreted as a **confirmation of a well-established indication** rather than a genuinely novel repurposing signal. The large body of completed Phase 3 RCTs identified below reflects decades of established clinical practice rather than emerging evidence for a new use.

Mechanistically, DNA alkylation is a broadly applicable cytotoxic mechanism across glial tumour subtypes, which is consistent with both the very high TxGNN prediction score (99.36%) and the depth of clinical evidence retrieved.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
A blocking data gap (missing label warnings/contraindications) prevents completion of the safety pre-assessment, and this dataset shows no confirmed UK marketing authorisation for temozolomide. In addition, "adult astrocytic tumour" substantially overlaps with the drug's already-established clinical use, so this result carries lower incremental repurposing value despite strong (L1) trial evidence.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- MHRA-approved SmPC / Patient Information Leaflet for temozolomide (warnings, contraindications, drug interactions)
- Confirmation of current UK marketing authorisation status and licence number(s)
- Clarification of the originally licensed indication, to properly assess whether this represents a novel repurposing opportunity or confirmation of existing use
