## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Pharmacology records show that indoramin binds the alpha-1A, alpha-1B and alpha-1D adrenoceptors, and it is known as a postsynaptic alpha-1 blocker that relaxes peripheral arterioles and lowers blood pressure.

No plausible mechanistic link between alpha-1 blockade and hypertrichosis has been identified. The prediction appears to be model-derived and may be confounded by other vasodilator drugs, such as minoxidil, which are associated with excess hair growth. In practical terms, this prediction should be regarded as a hypothesis generated by the model, not as a rationale-supported repurposing opportunity.
