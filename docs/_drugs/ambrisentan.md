## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data are not available in the data compiled for this report. Ambrisentan belongs to the endothelin receptor antagonist class, which acts on endothelin-mediated vasoconstriction and vascular remodelling.

The link to pulmonary arteriovenous malformation is weak. These lesions are structural vascular anomalies, most often seen in hereditary haemorrhagic telangiectasia (HHT), and are not mainly driven by endothelin-mediated vasoconstriction. The one linked publication describes pulmonary arterial hypertension occurring alongside HHT. This suggests ambrisentan might treat the pulmonary hypertension component rather than the malformation itself. Vasodilators may also worsen right-to-left shunting, which is a theoretical safety concern.

The high TxGNN score is therefore not backed by a clear mechanistic rationale and should be treated as a hypothesis only.
