## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Bicalutamide is, however, a recognised non-steroidal androgen receptor antagonist.

Hair growth in many body sites is influenced by androgen signalling. Hypertrichosis induced by minoxidil in female pattern hair loss might plausibly be modulated by anti-androgen action, which gives a credible but unproven mechanistic rationale.

The single linked publication is a comment on a retrospective review of bicalutamide for minoxidil-induced hypertrichosis. The design and results of that underlying study are not included in the data, so they cannot be assessed here. Because no cited study directly and substantively addresses this disease, the evidence level is held at L5 (model prediction with no interpretable supporting study).
