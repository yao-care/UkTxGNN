## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available for ammonia, so the high score cannot be traced to a biological rationale.

Acrodermatitis chronica atrophicans is a late-stage skin manifestation of *Borrelia* infection and is treated with antibiotics. There is no known role for ammonia in its pathogenesis or treatment. The prediction most likely reflects the model's association of ammonia with other skin conditions rather than any pharmacological effect. On the current data, the prediction should be regarded as a hypothesis only and not as a plausible repurposing rationale.
