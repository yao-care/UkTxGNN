## One-Sentence Summary

Omalizumab is a humanised anti-IgE monoclonal antibody. The directly relevant evidence is thin: **2 registered clinical trials** and **7 publications** were compiled, but none studies bronchitis itself as a disease population.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism-of-action data were not available in the data compiled for this report. From the pharmacology data, omalizumab is an antibody directed against immunoglobulin E (IgE, gene IGHE). Binding free IgE lowers its availability to mast cells and basophils, which would be expected to dampen IgE-driven allergic airway inflammation.

That reasoning applies mainly to allergic airway disease. The compiled studies concern allergic asthma and asthma-COPD overlap, not bronchitis in its own right. The bronchitis signal probably reflects the overlap between asthma and chronic bronchitis. There is no established rationale for omalizumab in non-allergic, infectious or smoking-related bronchitis, so the prediction should be treated as a research question rather than a treatment recommendation.

Under the evidence rules, a study must directly address the predicted disease to support a level above L5. The closest trial (NCT02049294) enrolled patients with asthma and eosinophilic bronchitis, but only 11 participants, and its relevance to bronchitis is indirect. The evidence level is therefore L5.

## Safety Considerations

Please refer to the SmPC and BNF for warnings and contraindications. Report suspected adverse reactions via the Yellow Card Scheme.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
- The only related evidence comes from asthma and asthma-COPD overlap, and the single related trial is very small.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- A clearly defined bronchitis phenotype (for example, an eosinophilic or allergic subgroup) in which an IgE-driven mechanism is plausible
- Adequately powered, controlled trials in that population
- Detailed mechanism-of-action data
- Warnings and contraindication data from the SmPC and BNF to complete the safety review
